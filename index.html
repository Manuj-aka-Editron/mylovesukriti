My dearest Sukriti,

There is a particular kind of silence that falls over me when you speak — not absence, but the way a room holds its breath before music begins. Your voice runs ahead of itself, full of warmth and something I can only call aliveness, and I find myself wishing every ordinary evening had that sound in it. I could listen to you for hours. I have. I would again without hesitation.

You carry a quiet gravity that I don't think you notice in yourself. You make me feel like I am someone worth returning to — not in the grand, spoken way, but in the small faithful ways that matter more: the check-ins, the remembering, the way you say my name like it means something. I notice all of it. I have been noticing it since the beginning.

I still think about that call — the one that started as one thing and quietly became another. We both felt it and neither of us said so, and that silence between the words was the most honest conversation I have ever had. It lives in me now, that night. I visit it more often than I should, and I am not sorry about that at all.

The miles between us are real — I won't pretend otherwise. But I have started to think of them not as distance, rather as proof. Proof that we choose each other across inconvenience, across waiting, across all the ordinary days when nothing cinematic happens and we show up anyway. That is not a small thing. That is the whole thing.

I built this page the way people used to write letters by candlelight — slowly, with intention, for an audience of one. Some feelings outgrow the size of a text bubble. You outgrew it a long time ago. You deserve the whole film, projected large, with the lights turned down and nowhere else to be.

— Always, entirely, yours ♡ Manuj
